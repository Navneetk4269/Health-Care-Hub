Who We Are

Bridging the Gap Between Patients & Providers

Healthcare Hub is a digital platform designed for B.E. IT students and modern healthcare innovators to connect service receivers (patients) directly with service providers (doctors, nurses, diagnostic labs). We simplify healthcare access with a clean, intuitive interface.

Get Started Today
🩺

Service Providers

Doctors, specialists, nurses, and labs can register, manage profiles, set availability, and reach more patients digitally.

🧑‍⚕️

Service Receivers

Patients can search providers, book appointments, view prescriptions, and track their health records securely.
